Questions parents ask

Which program is right for my kid?
Fun with AI is about creative projects: a character, story, comic, song, and game. Learn with AI focuses on research, creating, and speaking skills for school and beyond. Same instructor, same safety-first approach either way, and plenty of kids do both.
Is coding needed?
No, for either program. If they can type and imagine, they're ready.
Is this safe for my child?
Yes. Safety and responsible AI use is built into every session in both programs, not a one-time lecture. Try our free AI Safety Chat to see the ground rules kids take home.
What age are these for?
Age 10+ for both programs. Basic English reading and typing is enough.
What device do they need?
A laptop is recommended for the best experience. A tablet or phone works but might be a hassle for some sessions.
Do they keep everything they create?
Yes, 100%. Everything they make is theirs, and both programs end with a certificate of completion.
What is the Fidgital Play kit?
Fidgital Play is a hands-on puzzle game for younger kids. The kit has a 5×5 board and fidget cubes to build with, plus a card with your kit code for the online game. Kids pick a puzzle on screen, build it with the cubes, and check their answer to earn stars.
How does the online game work?
Open the game and enter the kit code from the card in your box. Pick a puzzle on screen, build it on the board with the cubes before the timer ends, then check your answer and earn stars. Play solo, or with two players in Race or Take Turns mode. A tablet works best, a laptop or phone works too, and it needs an internet connection to load.

Ready to make something real?

Pick a live program, or start with the game kit. Not sure which? Start with the one that sounds like your child.
