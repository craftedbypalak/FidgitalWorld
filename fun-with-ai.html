The Journey

Start with a blank screen.
End with six things they made.

2 sessions, about 4 hours total. Run it as one focused day, or split into two easier halves. Tap any stop to peek inside.

Session 1: Imagine & Design (about 2 hrs)

🤖AI Basics & Safety20 min

A friendly, no-boring-lecture intro to how AI actually works.

  • What AI can (and honestly can't) do
  • Ground rules for privacy, fact-checking & spotting fakes
  • Getting comfortable writing their first prompts
📖Character & Story60 min

Meet the character they'll bring to life all session.

  • Design an original AI character: look, personality, powers
  • Write and illustrate their storybook
  • Make the calls that shape who this character is
💥Comic Strip40 min

Their story, remixed into 4 punchy panels.

  • Pick the story's most exciting moment
  • Turn it into a 4-panel comic strip
  • Add dialogue, action, and personality

Session 2: Build & Bring to Life (about 2 hrs)

🤲Finger Puppet30 min

From the screen to something they can actually hold.

  • Turn their character into a printable design
  • Cut, fold, and assemble a real paper puppet
  • Bring the digital character into the physical world
🎵Theme Song40 min

Every great character needs a theme song.

  • Write lyrics that capture their character's vibe
  • Generate a full song with AI music tools, vocals included
  • Pick a style: pop, epic, silly, anything goes
🎮Build a Game50 min

The big finale: a real, playable game.

  • Design a simple game starring their character
  • Build it with AI, genuinely no coding required
  • Play it, break it, fix it, play again
