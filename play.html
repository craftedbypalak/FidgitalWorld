How it works

Three simple steps, over and over

The screen sets the challenge. The real fun happens on the table.

1

Pick a puzzle on screen

Choose a level and the game shows a pattern on a 5×5 grid. That is what your child will build.

2

Build it with the cubes

3

Check it and earn stars

The game reveals the answer. Your child compares, taps "I got it!" or "Not quite", and collects stars as they go.

Ways to play

Alone, or side by side

Play solo to level up, or bring a sibling or a parent into the game.

Single player

Level up with stars

Pick a level, build each puzzle, and collect up to three stars.

  • Clear a level to unlock the next one.
  • Your best result is saved on the device.
  • Play again any time to earn more stars.
Dual player

Race

Both players get the same puzzle and the same timer, and build at the same time.

  • Each player builds on their own set.
  • Each correct build scores a point, and the first correct Done! earns a speed point too.
  • Everyone gets cheered at the end, even in a tie.
Dual player

Take Turns

One set of cubes, shared. One player builds while the other watches and cheers.

  • The watcher sees the answer and marks the build.
  • Then you swap, with a brand new puzzle.
  • Perfect for a parent and child, or two siblings.

New to fidget cubes? Learn to Play is a short, friendly guide inside the game that shows kids how the cubes and the board work before their first puzzle.

Questions parents ask

Where do I find the kit code?
On the card in your box. Open the online game, type the code in, and you are ready to play. Capital letters, small letters and dashes all work.
Which device should we use?
A tablet works best, because the game is laid out for a tablet screen. A laptop or a phone works too.
Does the game need internet?
Yes. You need internet to load the game. The board and cubes are right there on your table.
Can we use one kit on more than one device?
Yes. Enter the same code on each device. Levels and stars are saved on the device you play on.
Which age is it for?
Fidgital Play is made for kids to build and play, and it is just as much fun as a family game.
Can two people play together?
Yes. Dual Player has two modes. In Race, both players build the same puzzle at the same time. In Take Turns, one player builds while the other watches, then you swap.
What if my child does not get a puzzle right?
Nothing scary. The game says things like "So close! Try again!" and moves on to the next puzzle. It is built to be encouraging, never to make a child feel they failed.
