Good habits kids practise

🔍
Double-check facts

AI can sound very sure and still be wrong. Kids learn to look things up.

🔒
Protect personal information

Photos, passwords and secrets stay private. It's always okay to say no.

🧠
Keep the thinking theirs

AI is a great helper for ideas, as long as the work is still their own.

🧑‍🤝‍🧑
Ask a grown-up

Real problems, feelings and health questions belong with real people.
